01 / About

Research, technology
and their impact.

I am a Professor of Computer Science at Luiss University in Rome, where I serve as Deputy Rector for Artificial Intelligence and Digital Skills and as Founding Director of the Research Center AI4Society.

My academic work spans theoretical and applied computer science. I am interested in the design and analysis of efficient algorithms, particularly for graphs and large-scale data, and in the opportunities and challenges created by artificial intelligence.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Columbia University back in 1991 and was a Research Staff Member at the IBM T.J. Watson Research Center. I am an EATCS Fellow.

05 / Teaching

From foundations
to applications.

My teaching activities cover core and emerging areas of computer science, including:

  • Algorithms
  • Artificial intelligence and machine learning
  • Deep learning and generative AI
  • Cybersecurity and data science
  • AI literacy and the societal implications of AI

I am particularly interested in interdisciplinary education that connects technical foundations with management, law, economics and public policy.
